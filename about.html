ABOUT US

Amber's Braids - Pretoria's Finest

Welcome to Amber's Braids! Based in Pretoria, we specialize in beautiful, neat and long-lasting braids. We make every client feel confident and beautiful. Quality work, affordable prices, and love in every braid!

Amber's Braids was founded in 2023 by Amber Koketso, a passionate hairstylist with a love for braiding in Pretoria.

At Amber's Braids, our mission is to empower individuals through the art of braiding, providing them with beautiful, durable styles that enhance their natural features and boost their confidence.
